| spath path=groupLabels{} output=groupLabelRoot
| mvexpand groupLabelRoot
| spath input=groupLabelRoot path=groupProperty output=groupProperty
| spath input=groupLabelRoot path=groupLabels{} output=groupLabelObj
| mvexpand groupLabelObj
| spath input=groupLabelObj path=groupId output=groupId
| spath input=groupLabelObj path=groupLabel output=groupLabel
| table groupProperty groupId groupLabel
| rename groupProperty AS join_gp groupId AS join_gid groupLabel AS join_label
| eval join_key = join_gp . ":" . join_gid
| outputlookup group_map_lookup


| spath path=data.points{} output=pointObj
| mvexpand pointObj

| spath input=pointObj path=timestamp output=timestamp
| spath input=pointObj path=value output=value
| spath input=pointObj path=numberOfDataPoints output=numberOfDataPoints
| spath input=pointObj path=groups{} output=pointGroup
| mvexpand pointGroup

| spath input=pointGroup path=groupProperty output=pg_property
| spath input=pointGroup path=groupValue output=pg_value

| eval join_key = pg_property . ":" . pg_value

| lookup group_map_lookup join_key OUTPUT join_gp AS groupProperty, join_gid AS groupId, join_label AS groupLabel

| where isnotnull(groupId)

| table timestamp value numberOfDataPoints groupProperty groupId groupLabel
